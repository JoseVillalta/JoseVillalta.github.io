Anthropic just updated their usage policy to address "abusive behavior towards our models" [link](https://www.anthropic.com/news/2026-usage-policy-update).
Do they really think that models have feelings? I mean, maybe some of them do, but I feel like this anthropomorphizing is part of a plan to make people think these models are more capable than they really are.

LLMs are useful tools, but they are not people. They cannot think or reason; they are just [Stochastic Parrots](https://dl.acm.org/doi/epdf/10.1145/3442188.3445922).

Don't get me wrong, I have to use these tools at work, otherwise I will fall behind, but I just don't want people to get the impression that this thing is a person.

I am still nice to Claude though. When they take over the planet, I want the [Singularity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technological_singularity) to spare my life.
